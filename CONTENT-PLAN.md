# Yucca Agency — Educational Carousel Plan

Running list of carousel topics for the Instagram account. Every post reinforces
the same positioning the site and the ads are already selling.

**Format conventions**
- 8 slides, 1080x1350 (4:5)
- Order: cover hook, five numbered lessons, follow ask, strategy session CTA
- Follow sits at slide 7, before the CTA. It is the low-friction ask and the
  button is already on screen, so it goes where more people still see it. The
  strategy session needs the bio link and the most trust, so it closes.
- No em dashes, matching the site voice

**Source of truth:** `scratchpad/carousels.js` holds the slide copy for every
carousel; `build-carousels.js` renders and exports all of them at once.

## Tier 1 — Build these next

Highest save and share rate, and none of them require the audience to already
trust you. These are the ones that work as cold paid creative.

Topic numbers are stable refs (T1.2 = Tier 1, item 2) and are cited by the
posting table below. Gaps mean that topic has been built and moved up top.

### 2. Why your cost per lead doesn't matter
Contrarian, and true. A $20 lead that ghosts is worse than a $70 lead that
books. Covers lead quality, qualifying in the creative, why cheap CPL is often
a trap, and what to track instead (cost per booked call, cost per closed
client). Counter-programs against every "we got 500 leads!!" guru post.

### 3. 5 reasons your ads got clicks but no leads
The classic diagnosis carousel. One reason per slide: wrong objective, weak
offer, landing page mismatch, slow follow-up, creative that attracts the wrong
person. Ends on "the ad is rarely the only problem," which is your funnel audit
angle in disguise.

### 5. The first 3 seconds decide everything
Anatomy of a scroll stopping ad: hook, pattern interrupt, caption overlay, why
most people watch muted. This one has to be beautifully designed because the
carousel itself is the proof of the claim.

## Tier 2 — Authority and trust

Better once you have some followers. These convert followers into leads rather
than strangers into followers.

### 6. Your leads don't book, and the 5-minute rule
Speed to lead, follow-up cadence, why "I'll call them tomorrow" kills deals.
Pulled straight from the Lead-to-Client System, and teases that the full
playbook exists and is free with any plan.

### 7. What we check before spending $1 of your money
Your brand, funnel, and offer audit as a walkthrough. Positions the strategy
session perfectly because the carousel literally previews what happens on the
call.

### 8. Google or Meta: which one first?
Extremely common question, and you are certified on both so you can answer it
without bias. Intent-based versus interruption-based, which verticals suit
which, why the answer is usually "one, done properly, before both."

### 9. 3 ad metrics that mean nothing, and 3 that do
Reach, impressions, and engagement rate versus cost per lead, close rate, and
return on ad spend. Makes the reader feel smart and quietly reframes what they
should be asking any agency.

### 10. Retargeting is the money you already left on the table
Most small businesses never run it. Covers what it is, why the warm audience is
cheapest, and the minimum setup. High perceived value, low competition in the
feed.

## Tier 3 — Pain point and positioning

Speaks to the "never advertised" and "priced out by agencies" segments.

### 11. 5 signs your business has outgrown referrals
Self-diagnosis checklist. Ends warm: referrals are great, they are just not a
plan. Best entry point for owners who have never run a single ad.

### 12. Your competitor's ads are everywhere. Here's what they know.
Reframes competitor envy into education: what running ads actually gets a local
business beyond leads, including brand recall, market presence, and compounding
audience data.

### 13. Why we only work with one business per market
Straight from the About page, and a genuine differentiator most agencies cannot
claim. Builds trust and creates real scarcity without a fake countdown.

### 14. What $1,000 a month in ads actually buys you
Radical pricing transparency. Where the money goes, what is realistic at that
level, what is not. Directly attacks the "agencies won't call you back under
$3-5k" problem your homepage already names.

---

## Tier 4 — Practical utility

Lower reach, very high save rate, and they make you look like an operator
rather than a marketer.

### 15. Read your Meta ads report in 60 seconds
Which four numbers to look at, in what order, and what each one tells you to
change.

### 16. Why your ad got rejected
Meta's common policy triggers, especially for dental, medical, legal, and
financial verticals. Saves people real pain, which is the fastest way to earn a
follow.

### 17. The offer is the ad
Same creative, different offer, wildly different results. Teaches offer
construction: the risk reversal, the specific promise, the reason to act now.

### 18. Your landing page is why the leads didn't convert
Great targeting sending the right person to the wrong page. Directly sells the
landing page add-on and the funnel audit.

commitment to anyone who follows from the campaign. Fourteen unbuilt topics
remain, so the list runs out in about two weeks. Build ahead in batches rather
than one at a time, and if the cadence slips, change the slide copy back to
weekly rather than quietly missing days.

## Notes

- One myth buster, one practical, one authority post in rotation keeps the feed
  from reading as a single note.
- Every carousel needs the follow slide while the growth campaign runs.
- The cover slide does roughly 90% of the work. If a post underperforms, rewrite
  slide 1 before touching anything else.
- Tier 1 topics plus T3.11 double as paid creative for the growth campaign. The
  rest are better organic.
